01

Information you use in Weight Lab

Weight Lab helps you compare candidate sites and alternatives using criteria, weights, judgments and analysis results. Information you enter or import can include project names, source attributes, candidate coordinates, notes, observations and evidence files.

These workspace contents are processed and stored on your device. Weight Lab does not automatically upload them to us. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics or automatic cloud synchronization.

Coordinates contained in an imported file are part of the data you choose to use; they do not mean that the app is tracking your device’s location.

02

Local storage and how it is used

Your workspace and preferences are saved locally so you can reopen studies, run comparisons, review results and keep your chosen settings between sessions.

The privacy viewer can also save the address and appearance settings of an online notice, allowing it to retry the last available notice if a later configuration request fails. This saved address is not a copy of your workspace and is not a complete offline website.

Local processing components support calculations, geometry and coordinate operations, database storage and file packaging. They are not advertising or analytics services.

03

Files, exports and backups

You choose the files you import and the reports, study packages or backups you export. Exported files can contain project names, coordinates, attributes, judgments, notes and analysis results. Some export options can also include original sources or evidence attachments.

When you use the system file picker or share sheet, the destination you select — such as a storage provider, another app or another person — may receive the selected files. Review the contents and destination before sharing. Weight Lab cannot retrieve or delete copies already sent elsewhere.

The app’s workspace is excluded from automatic system cloud backup. Use Weight Lab’s backup controls when you want to preserve a copy, and keep that copy in a location you trust. Choosing a cloud folder for an export or backup is your own file-sharing action, not automatic synchronization by Weight Lab.

04

Online notices and external services

The core analysis tools work locally. Online privacy delivery can nevertheless make requests to check connectivity, retrieve notice settings or display an online policy. These requests can reach Apple connectivity or lookup services, a configuration service and the site hosting the notice.

The receiving services can receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, request time and standard browser or network headers. The privacy-delivery requests do not include your study contents. Hosting services may maintain operational logs under their own policies.

If you open an external reference or another website, that service handles the resulting connection under its own privacy policy. Embedded web content may also use cookies or local website storage; Weight Lab’s privacy viewer does not clear all such site data on every visit. This does not add advertising or analytics to Weight Lab’s local analysis tools.

05

Retention, deletion and security

Workspace information remains in the app’s local storage until you delete it, replace it through restore or reset operations, or remove the app and its local data. You can manage studies and local data using the app’s controls. Removing local information does not remove backups, exports or attachments already saved outside the app.

Saved privacy-viewer settings remain until they are updated, cleared or removed with the app’s data. Because we do not receive your local workspace automatically, we cannot recover or remotely erase it for you.

Weight Lab uses the device’s app-storage protections. Protect your device and any exported backups, particularly when your files contain personal or confidential information. No storage or transmission method can provide an absolute security guarantee.

06

Your choices and contacting us

You decide what information to enter, which files to import and whether to export or share anything. The app’s local analysis does not depend on creating an account or accepting advertising or analytics tracking.

Continue to App means close this notice. The button does not record consent to data collection, accept terms or grant a system permission. You do not have to scroll to the end to use it.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your question or request and retain it as needed for that purpose and applicable legal obligations. Please avoid sending confidential project files unless they are necessary for your request.

You may contact us about access, correction or deletion of information you have sent to us, or about other privacy rights that apply to you. Local workspace data can be managed directly in the app.

07

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Weight Lab’s features or information practices change. The date at the top identifies the current revision. An online copy may be updated separately from the copy included in an installed app.

We will explain material changes through an appropriate notice. Where a change requires your permission, we will request it separately; continuing past this reading screen is not a substitute for that permission.
